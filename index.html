Storefront offline

The Jitney

Catalog temporarily paused while the storefront backend connection is completed.

The product catalog is intentionally offline until the Make backend is fully connected to the front-end storefront.

  • No products are being displayed while the pipeline is being completed.
  • Checkout is disabled until the backend route is live and verified.
  • The storefront will return once the catalog and purchase flow are synchronized.

This is a temporary maintenance state to prevent broken product flow and incomplete customer transactions.
